# Viral Opportunity Scoring Rubric

## Base score, 100 points

| Dimension | Weight | What good looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience fit | 20 | Clearly matches the target persona in the brief |
| Pain urgency | 15 | Addresses a real decision, fear, bottleneck, or high-intent need |
| Frequency/repetition | 15 | Appears across multiple sources or repeatedly in comments/questions |
| Shareability/saveability | 10 | Useful enough to save, forward, or discuss |
| Novelty/angle gap | 10 | Not just another generic roadmap; has a clear new angle/localization |
| MindX positioning fit | 15 | Can naturally connect to MindX POV, curriculum, proof, or CTA |
| Conversion potential | 10 | Likely to attract leads worth follow-up, not just casual traffic |
| Source credibility | 5 | Supported by credible or multiple directional sources |

## Penalties

| Risk | Penalty |
|---|---:|
| High copyright/copy risk | -20 |
| Unsupported major claim required | -15 |
| Generic/saturated topic with no differentiating angle | -10 |
| Outdated or unstable information without current source | -10 |
| Weak connection to MindX offer | -20 |
| Hype-heavy or ethically risky framing | -15 |

## Format recommendation guide

| Signal | Best format |
|---|---|
| “Học gì trước?” | Roadmap or decision tree |
| “Tôi có hợp không?” | Diagnostic checklist or quiz |
| “Làm thế nào trong công việc?” | Workflow guide or template pack |
| “Cần project gì?” | Portfolio/project kit |
| “Prompt/tool nào dùng được?” | Prompt pack or tool workflow kit |
| “Phỏng vấn cần gì?” | Interview prep kit |

## Tie-breakers

If two ideas score similarly, choose the one that:
1. Has stronger MindX proof/CTA bridge.
2. Can be made into a tangible asset quickly.
3. Has lower copyright and fact-check risk.
4. Creates follow-up content for social/email/admission team.
