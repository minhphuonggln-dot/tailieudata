## Common persona segments for MindX lead magnets

### Data

- Student exploring Data Analyst as first career.
- Working professional with Excel/reporting exposure considering Data.
- Marketer/finance/ops staff who wants to become more data-driven.
- Beginner trying to build portfolio and interview confidence.

### ITBA

- Non-tech professional considering BA/ITBA transition.
- Fresher BA who needs project/workflow templates.
- Developer/QA wanting to move closer to business/product.
- Student comparing BA, ITBA, PO, PM paths.

### AI

- Office worker wanting AI productivity without deep coding.
- Student wanting AI literacy for employability.
- Team lead/manager exploring AI workflows safely.
- Data/BA learner wanting to add AI to existing workflow.

## Objection bank

- “Tôi trái ngành có học được không?”
- “Không giỏi toán/coding thì có theo được không?”
- “Nên học tool nào trước?”
- “AI có làm role này bị thay thế không?”
- “Học xong có làm được project thật không?”
- “Tôi không biết bắt đầu từ đâu.”
- “Có quá nhiều khóa/tài liệu, không biết chọn gì.”

## Conversion implications

Tie CTA to the reader's next step:
- If reader is exploring: CTA to diagnostic/consultation/webinar.
- If reader is comparing paths: CTA to roadmap consultation or curriculum preview.
- If reader wants templates: CTA to workshop/project-based class.
- If reader wants career proof: CTA to portfolio review or admissions consult.
