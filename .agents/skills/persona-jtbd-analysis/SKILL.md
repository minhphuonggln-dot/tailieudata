# Persona JTBD Analysis

## Objective

Turn the selected lead magnet idea into a precise audience strategy so the draft speaks to a specific person, moment, and decision.

## Required inputs

- Selected lead magnet idea from `02_idea_shortlist.md`
- Research digest and source log
- Campaign brief
- Track: Data, ITBA, AI, or mixed

## Workflow

1. **Identify the decision moment.** Define what the audience is trying to decide or accomplish now.
2. **Create persona snapshot.** Summarize current state, desired state, constraints, trigger, skill level, and context.
3. **Write the JTBD.** Use: “When I..., I want to..., so I can...”
4. **Map jobs.** Separate functional, emotional, and social jobs.
5. **Map pains/gains.** Connect each pain/gain to a content response in the lead magnet.
6. **Capture objections.** Include objections about time, cost, difficulty, tools, career risk, background, AI hype, or job relevance.
7. **Build language bank.** Use paraphrased audience wording from public sources; do not copy private personal details.
8. **Define conversion implication.** Recommend CTA, lead qualification question, and follow-up angle.

## Output format

Create/update `production_artifacts/03_persona_jtbd.md` with:

- persona snapshot,
- JTBD statement,
- jobs map,
- pain/gain map,
- objections,
- language bank,
- conversion implication.

## Quality checklist

- Persona is specific enough to guide writing.
- JTBD ties to a real decision or progress moment.
- Objections are not generic; they reflect research signals.
- Language bank sounds like the target audience, not internal marketing jargon.
- CTA recommendation follows naturally from the reader's goal.
