# MindX Positioning Framework

## Message house template

| Layer | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| One-liner promise | What practical value will the reader get? | 1 sentence |
| Main argument | What does MindX believe about this topic? | 1-2 paragraphs |
| Supporting point 1 | What should the reader understand first? | Practical point |
| Supporting point 2 | What should the reader do next? | Actionable point |
| Supporting point 3 | Why is MindX credible/helpful here? | Proof-backed point |
| CTA bridge | What is the logical next step? | Soft CTA |

## Generic-to-MindX transformation

Generic content:
- “Here are 20 AI tools.”

MindX-owned version:
- “Here are 5 AI workflows mapped to real work situations, with prompts, safety checks, and a 7-day practice plan.”

Generic content:
- “Learn SQL, Power BI, Python.”

MindX-owned version:
- “Decide what to learn first based on your current background, target role, and the portfolio proof you need.”

Generic content:
- “Business Analyst roadmap.”

MindX-owned version:
- “Understand the exact conversations, documents, and decisions an ITBA handles in a software project.”

## Claim safety ladder

Strong claim requires proof:
- “X% of learners get jobs.”
- “Average salary is Y.”
- “MindX guarantees...”

Safer phrasing if proof is missing:
- “This guide helps you clarify...”
- “You can use this checklist to assess...”
- “A practical next step is...”

## CTA patterns

- Diagnostic CTA: “Muốn biết mình nên bắt đầu từ Data/ITBA/AI như thế nào? Hãy làm bài tư vấn lộ trình...”
- Workshop CTA: “Nếu bạn muốn thực hành workflow này cùng mentor, tham khảo workshop...”
- Curriculum CTA: “Nếu bạn cần một lộ trình học có cấu trúc, xem chương trình...”
- Portfolio CTA: “Nếu bạn muốn biến checklist này thành project thật, đăng ký nhận tư vấn...”
