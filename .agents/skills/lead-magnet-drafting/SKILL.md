# Lead Magnet Drafting

## Objective

Create a complete, useful, source-backed lead magnet draft in Vietnamese that reflects MindX positioning and gives the reader a practical next step.

## Required inputs

- `00_brief.md`
- `01_research_digest.md`
- `02_idea_shortlist.md`
- `03_persona_jtbd.md`
- `04_positioning_brief.md`
- `source_log.csv`
- Brand inputs

## Workflow

1. **Read upstream artifacts.** Identify selected idea, persona, promise, MindX POV, CTA, and risks.
2. **Choose the asset format.** Ebook, checklist, roadmap, workbook, prompt pack, template pack, quiz, or hybrid.
3. **Create title variants.** Generate 5-10 titles with different hooks: clarity, speed, confidence, template, career decision, anti-hype.
4. **Draft outline.** Build sections around reader progress, not internal curriculum.
5. **Write the lead magnet.** Use clear Vietnamese, explain necessary English terms, and add examples relevant to learners in Vietnam.
6. **Add utility blocks.** Include checklist, worksheet, decision tree, template, prompt pack, project brief, or exercise wherever useful.
7. **Add CTA.** Place CTA naturally after value, not before value.
8. **Add source notes.** List source refs/titles used for support. Do not paste long source excerpts.
9. **Flag unresolved issues.** Mark claims needing proof, missing data, or sections needing SME review.

## Output format

Create/update:

- `production_artifacts/05_outline.md`
- `production_artifacts/06_draft.md`

Draft structure:

1. Title
2. Subtitle
3. Short landing page description
4. Who this is for
5. Who this is not for
6. How to use this guide
7. Opening hook
8. Core sections
9. Checklist/template/workbook section
10. Summary
11. CTA to MindX
12. Source notes

## Writing rules

- Default language: Vietnamese.
- Keep terminology practical and concrete.
- Use tables/checklists when they improve usability.
- Avoid hype and unsupported promises.
- Do not write a brochure. Teach first, then bridge to MindX.
- Do not copy source wording or structure.

## Quality checklist

- The reader can do something specific after reading.
- The draft reflects the selected persona and JTBD.
- MindX angle is visible but not pushy.
- Claims are source-backed or flagged.
- The asset is design-ready enough for handoff.
