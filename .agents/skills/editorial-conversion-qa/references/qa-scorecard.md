# QA Scorecard

## Dimension scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

### 1. Source support

1 = major claims unsupported.
3 = most claims supported but some ambiguity.
5 = major claims traceable to source log, brand inputs, or clearly marked opinion.

### 2. Copyright/originality

1 = closely copies a source or competitor.
3 = mostly original but structure/phrasing may be too derivative.
5 = clearly synthesized and transformed into a MindX-owned asset.

### 3. Persona fit

1 = generic audience.
3 = generally relevant but could be sharper.
5 = speaks to a specific moment, pain, language, and decision.

### 4. MindX positioning

1 = could belong to any brand.
3 = has CTA but weak POV/proof.
5 = clear MindX POV, proof plan, safe claims, natural CTA.

### 5. Practical usefulness

1 = informational only.
3 = some tips but limited utility.
5 = includes actionable checklist/template/workflow/exercise.

### 6. Conversion clarity

1 = no CTA or irrelevant CTA.
3 = CTA exists but could be sharper.
5 = CTA is contextual, helpful, and tied to reader progress.

### 7. Readability

1 = dense, confusing, jargon-heavy.
3 = understandable but needs structure.
5 = clear, skimmable, concrete examples.

### 8. Design readiness

1 = hard to design.
3 = some structure but not visual-ready.
5 = clear headings, callouts, tables, worksheets, and visual notes.

## Status rules

- Pass: no critical issues, most scores 4-5.
- Pass with edits: no critical issues, only minor/major fixes.
- Needs revision: major structural/content issues.
- Blocked: any critical issue unresolved.

## Common fixes

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Unsupported salary/job claim | Remove, source, or rewrite as non-guaranteed guidance |
| Too generic | Add persona-specific situation, decision, example |
| Too salesy | Move CTA later and add more value before CTA |
| Weak utility | Add checklist/template/workflow/exercise |
| Too derivative | Rebuild around MindX POV and original structure |
| Jargon-heavy | Add plain Vietnamese explanation and example |
